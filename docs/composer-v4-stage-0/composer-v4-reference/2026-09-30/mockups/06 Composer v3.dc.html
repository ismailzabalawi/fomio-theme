Composer · v3 (superseded by v4)

The same native Discourse composer, reorganised around one question: what do I want to share? Where it goes and who can read it sit at the top. The title and body take the screen. Three labelled entry points (Photo, Format, More) replace the row of formatting icons, and the strip changes with what you have selected. Draft state is always stated truthfully, and one action matches your intent.
